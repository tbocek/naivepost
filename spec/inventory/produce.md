## A. Produce UI

Horizontal paned at half: left = the images/thumbnail column; right (min 360) = the written column (title, description), a separator, then the encoder settings in a scroller.
Encoder settings heading "Transcode" ("How the finished video is encoded, and where it goes: produce/final, beside everything else this step writes") with ↻ Transcode ("Encode the video again from the cut and these settings — no model call, and the thumbnail and the upload text are left alone") and ⤓ Save video ("Save the finished video somewhere else — a copy; produce/final stays where it is").

| Setting | Options | Default | Tooltip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Container | mp4, mkv, webm | mp4 | mp4 plays everywhere; mkv keeps subtitle tracks best; webm forces VP9 + Opus |
| Codec | h264, h265, vp9 | h264 | h264 is the safe upload; h265 is smaller but slower; vp9 is for webm |
| Preset | ultrafast, veryfast, fast, medium, slow, veryslow | veryslow | how long the encoder may think — slower means smaller at the same quality |
| Resolution | 720p, 1080p, original | 1080p | the short side of the frame — the cut page's aspect sets its shape; original keeps the footage's own size |
| Frame rate | source, 60, 30, 24 | 30 | output frame rate — a ceiling rather than a target with VFR on |
| Audio | 128, 192, 256, 320 | 128 | audio bitrate in kbit/s |
| Subtitles | burned in, track in file, none in the video | none | what the VIDEO carries… An .srt is written beside the video either way — one per language ticked… |
| Translate | menu of ticks: English, German, French (the session's own language hidden) | none | Also write the subtitles in these languages, translated… |
| Game audio | 0..1 step 0.02 | 0.22 | how loud the original game audio sits under the narration (hidden when narration is off) |
| Quality (CRF) | 14..34, mark 24 | 24 | quality: lower is better and bigger (18–24 is the usual range; 24 is the default, marked) |
| Frame timing | tick "peak rate (VFR)" | off | Treat the frame rate above as a ceiling… |
| Channels | tick "mono" | off | Mix the finished audio down to a single channel… |
| Frame edges | tick "blurred" | on | Fill the empty edges of the frame with a blown-up, blurred copy of the picture itself, instead of black… |

Output file is not a question: produce/final.<container>. webm forces vp9 and turns "track in file" into "none".
Inputs row: "N clip(s) · mm:ss" [· no narration | · N to speak] [· no upload text]; "no cut yet — build one on the Cut step"; tooltip lists cut.json, narration lines and cache state, mixed recordings, the voice, and whether publish.json exists. Outputs: folder "produce/ — the finished video, the per-clip encodes, the thumbnail and the upload text" + summary; tooltip "<file> — size, age".
Thumbnail column: heading "Images" ("What the image model is given, in order. The FIRST is the base — the picture being edited — and the others are references the instruction can name. 3 are taken from the cut the first time this page runs; after that the row is yours…") + "Add image…"; a row of up to 8 slots (picture 200 px, name, "Make base", "Set Thumbnail" ("Put this picture on the thumbnail as it is -- cropped to the video's shape, no model and no GPU…"), "Change…", remove); empty state "No image — the thumbnail will be drawn from the instruction alone.\nAdd image… to edit one of your own frames instead."; the base slot wears a crop box when its aspect differs ("The part of this frame the thumbnail keeps — the video's own shape. Drag it over what matters…"). Heading "Edit instruction" (4-line box; "What the image model is told to make out of the images… No words: the title and the marked texts are printed on afterwards…"); "Negative prompt" (2 lines; "What must stay out of the picture — watermarks, logos, lettering, extra limbs."); "Thumbnail" with ⤓ export ("Export the thumbnail as a JPEG — what YouTube takes, under its 2 MB limit…") and ↻ redraw ("Draw the thumbnail again from the images and the instruction as they stand — a fresh draw…"); the result picture (min 120) with a text overlay editor ("Drag a box over the picture to put words on it — they are printed to fill the box, like a Text effect in Cut. Drag a box's border to resize it or its middle to move it, and press its ✎ to reword or remove it. The title is printed across the top on its own.").
Written column: "Title" heading with ↻ suggest ("Ask the model for a fresh title, thumbnail instruction and description — the only thing that does…"); title entry (placeholder "the video's title, also printed on the thumbnail — ▶ suggests one"; tooltip: the first thumbnail takes it as its printed line; after that the two are separate; four to seven words); "YouTube description" heading + 8-line box.
Text overlays: dashed violet boxes with a ✎ chip (22 px); snap to picture edges/middles and other boxes (10 px); min box 28 px; ✎ dialog "Words on the thumbnail" (Remove / Cancel / Save); the title box defaults to {0.5, 0.25, 1, 0.4} and edits the printed title only.
<video> tag: written after both halves; poster <stem>.jpg (JPEG 90) from thumbnail.png; tracks = the .vtt files on disk (own language first, default); `<video poster controls src preload="none"><track label kind="subtitles" srclang src [default]/></video>`; file <stem>.html; log ">>> the <video> tag for it: …"; notes for mkv/h265 (not web-playable) and for file:// subtitle refusal.

## B. Render flow

▶ = produceClicked: if the render is stale run at once (words=true), else ask "Overwrite <base>?" ("<path> — size, age\n\nThe encode takes minutes and there is no undo for it."). ↻ Transcode always asks, words=false.
produceRun: refuse when busy; no segs → "no cut yet — build one on the Cut step first"; snapshot on the GUI thread (segs, entries, settings, sources, publish state, aspect, whether publish.json exists); save; startRun. encode = !words || stale (">>> the video is already what this page describes — not encoding it again (↻ beside Transcode encodes anyway)"). Opening lines: ">>> transcoding %s: N clips at container/codec crf N — the thumbnail and the upload text are left as they are" | ">>> producing …, and the thumbnail redrawn beside them" | "…, and the upload text and thumbnail written beside them". Two halves in parallel: publishStage (its own progress line; failure logged "!!! the upload text and thumbnail failed: … -- the render carries on") and produce; then writeEmbed; the render's error wins; end: "produce FAILED: …" / "production failed — see log" / "production stopped" / "done" + ">>> <file>  (X s, size)" + "produced <file> — X s, size".
Clips: produceSegs = splitSpliced(cut) with scene numbers then applyFx (rate boundaries). sessionTracks builds the videos and lanes on the session clock with shifts, further tracks and cut lanes, rows assigned.
produce(): 0 scratch produce/clips cleared; 1 speak missing lines (job "speaking" 1/2; "synthesis for Ns: …" on failure); 2 plan clips: copies (source second; "clip N copies footage at T s that falls in no recording — skipped"; shortened past the recording's end), sound inserts (needs a picture; "…its sound has no picture, skipped"), inserts ("clip N: <file> is not there any more — skipped"), footage (rate, clamped to the recording); clips < 0.5 s dropped ("clip N at m:ss is X s — too short to render, dropped — the narration line(s) written on it are dropped with it"); narration placed and fitted (lead 0.3, gap 0.3, tail 0.2, grow ≤ 4 s, slide earlier, speed ≤ 1.25×; logs "clip N: the narration does not fit where it was placed — moved X s earlier" / "clip N: narration X s does not fit Y s — sped up Zx" / "clip N: no synthesis for a line — it is captioned only" / "clip N at T s has no narration entry — it keeps its own audio"); no clip → "no clip could be placed on a recording"; audio plan; lane mixes (overlap ≥ 0.1 s; per-lane report "<lane> is mixed into N of the M clips" / "…every one of them leaves it out — it is not in the render" / "…was not running while any clip was"); frame box for every clip; camera per footage clip (zoom > 10× logged); text/gain/hush cues; stop stills from the scene's camera. 3 subtitles built on the produced clock (final.srt in clips/; stale sidecars deleted by exact name first). 4 encode each clip (job "clip" queue; stems c%03d_<stamp> or c%03d_<safe insert stem>; burned cues per clip; ffprobe the result for joinMismatch "…the join is a stream copy and cannot mix sizes, so the video breaks at this clip"; concat.txt). 5 join by stream copy (0.92 "joining"). 6 translate after the encodes (0.94 "translating"). 7 loudness + mux (0.96): -map 0:v -map 0:a -c:v copy -af aresample=async=1:first_pts=0,loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1.5:LRA=11 -ar 48000, audio codec, subtitle tracks (mov_text for mp4, srt otherwise; not webm), mp4 +faststart+negative_cts_offsets -use_editlist 0; "!!! nothing to put in a subtitle: no narration, and no speech in the clips" when no cue; webm note. 8 sidecars <stem>[.code].srt/.vtt per track (">>> subtitles: X.srt and .vtt").
encodeClip inputs (order is load-bearing): 0 picture (-ss before -i; freeze -t 0.5; insert video/svg baked frames/still loop); 1 sound (inserted sound, own-clock read, or anullsrc when the picture carries none); voices (one per spoken line); mixes (one per lane overlap); text overlays (-loop 1 -framerate, svg sized into its box, -t length+0.2); stills (-ss at -t 0.5).
Video chain: setpts/speed → freeze trim+tpad → fps (fixed, or the camera's under VFR) → stills overlays → backdrop (blurred: split, scale increase+crop+gblur sigma max(4, 0.02·h), overlay centred; bare: pad black) → camera/fit/scale → insert tpad → setsar=1 → subtitles=file → text overlays.
Audio chain: speed (atempo chain or asetrate) → hush volume=0 enable → apad when needed → lane bed amix normalize=0 → gain volume expressions → seam dips → narration: per line atempo≥0.5, aresample 48000, pan mono/stereo, adelay whole ms; bg volume=GameVol; amix duration=first normalize=0 → alimiter=limit=0.891:level=disabled → format fltp/48000/layout. Output -t for inserts/sounds/freezes/rated clips; fps args (-fps_mode cfr | -fpsmax N | -fps_mode vfr); codecs (libx264 preset crf yuv420p -refs 4; libx265 -tag:v hvc1; libvpx-vp9 -crf -b:v 0 -row-mt 1 -cpu-used by preset); audio aac|libopus -b:a Nk -ac 1|2.
Checkpoints: per line, per clip, before join, before translate/mux, before the publish model call.
Render stamp: sha1 of {settings without OutFile, segs, lines (S,E,text,wav size@mtime), sources (path size@mtime), aspect, voice, narrOff} at <stem>.stamp; stale when missing/mismatched; not covering the upload text; "written" for text = publish.json exists.

## C. Subtitles

Cues per clip from the narration lines when present, else from the clip's own speech: words fully inside the clip (narrator mic excluded), glued/dressed/respelled from the fixed transcript (fixWords), per WORD: flush at gaps ≥ 0.6 s, > 84 chars, or ≥ 6 s; folded spellings keep seconds; an empty cue extends the previous instead. Timeline assembly per clip on the produced clock; tidyCues: no overlaps, gaps < 1.2 s held, cues < 0.8 s folded into the next, last extended to 0.8. wrapSub 42 chars/row, max 2 rows. Placement tags {\an8} top, {\an5} centre. srt/vtt times; vtt escapes.
Translation: languages en/eng/English, de/deu/German, fr/fra/French; track 0 always the session's language (final.srt); per wanted language: ">>> subtitles: translating N lines into X"; user = context block + "TRANSLATE THESE N LINES INTO X. Answer with N lines, numbered as they are here:\n\n<n\ttext>"; thinking off; cache hit ">>> subtitles: X came from the cache…"; failure "!!! subtitles: X: … -- that language is left out"; reply read back by number (tab or first space; blanks missing); gaps re-asked once with original numbers ("They are lines of a longer track, so their numbers do not start at 1…"), uncached; still missing keep the original ("!!! subtitles: X: N line(s) left in the original (…) -- the rest of the track is good"); cached only when complete. Files final.<code>.srt in clips/, sidecars beside the video.

## D. Publish flow

publishBrief: "THE FINISHED VIDEO: N clips, m:ss long.\n\nWHAT IS IN EACH CLIP:\n" + per clip "CLIP n (at m:ss in the video, X s): session a–b" + the clip's lines/events; then "THE NARRATION SPOKEN OVER IT, at its time in the finished video:\n  [m:ss] (clip n) text" or "(no narration has been written for this video)".
writeUploadAt: system = youtube prompt; user = context block + brief; checkpoint; web_search/web_read tools when firefox is available (not Flatpak); llmChatRetryTools thinking ON; reply peeled for "TITLE:", "THUMBNAIL:" (either an instruction or "frame: clip <n> +<seconds>"), the rest = description (a short "Description:" lead-in line dropped); empty description → "the model answered with nothing". frameSecs: "clip N [at] [+off]" → segs[N-1].S + off; else plain seconds or mm:ss.
publishStage(needText, written, textOnly, force): deadlock breaker (no picture, no images, no instruction and no thumbnail → ask for the text again); needText → brief, log context, progress "writing the title, the instruction and the description", call; a frame answer → takeFrameAt (nearest extracted frame; cropped to the video's shape; Own=true; log "the thumbnail is the frame at m:ss, as it is — no model, no GPU"); missing title/instruction leave the previous value; description replaced; log "publish: title %q, instruction N characters, description N characters"; land on the GUI; first run with no images and not Own → take 3 evenly spread frames from kept footage ("publish: no images chosen — taking 3 from the cut"); write publish.json + description.txt; seed the printed title once; Own → print words only ("the thumbnail is a chosen picture — not redrawing it (↻ over it draws)"); textOnly → stop; not force and not stale → print words only; else progress 0.5 "drawing the thumbnail", draw, write thumbnail.stamp (sha1 of frames+marks, prompt, negative, crop, aspect, Own).
Drawing: base missing → error; instruction = prompt + "Do not write any words, letters, titles, logos or captions into the picture. Keep the <upper|middle|lower> part of the picture calm and uncluttered: a title will be printed across it afterwards."; empty prompt → "nothing to tell the image model — write an edit instruction first (▶ suggests one)"; frame = pubBox(aspect) (long side 1280, even); base cropped, references raw as data URLs; sd request {prompt, negative_prompt, width, height, seed −1, ref_images, auto_resize_ref_image true, output_format png} (no steps/cfg: server defaults); POST /sdcpp/v1/img_gen (60 s) → job id; poll GET /sdcpp/v1/jobs/<id> every 1 s (30 s timeout) with progress "drawing (<status>[, N ahead in the queue])"; completed → images[0].b64_json; failed/cancelled/404 errors; stop → cancel. Result thumbnail-plain.png; words printed onto thumbnail.png (marked texts then the title; failure keeps the plain picture). Capabilities GET /sdcpp/v1/capabilities (15 s) → model name; canImage = img_gen among modes or none reported.
Page runs: ↻ redraw (Own cleared, force) and ↻ suggest (textOnly) each a run of their own; statuses "thumbnail drawn — ▶ renders the video" / "title, instruction and description rewritten — ▶ renders the video"; export as JPEG under 2 MB (qualities 92, 85, 75, 60, 40).
publish.json: {frames[], base (legacy), crop{x,y}, own, title_box{cx,cy,wf,hf}, thumb_title, title_seeded, title_off (legacy), texts[{cx,cy,wf,hf,text}], title, prompt, negative, description}; plus description.txt, thumbnail.png, thumbnail-plain.png, thumbnail.stamp under produce/publish/ (legacy <project>/publish/ honoured).

## E. LLM client

Endpoint: POST <server>/v1/chat/completions, bearer key; server from the box (https:// added when no scheme) or `http://127.0.0.1:8731;` model required ("no LLM model configured -- use the gear button"). Models: GET /v1/models (15 s).
Body: model, messages, top_p 0.95, top_k 20, min_p 0, presence_penalty 0; thinking: temperature 1.0, max_tokens 65536, enable_thinking true; execute: temperature 0.6, max_tokens 8192, enable_thinking false; the thinking switch sent both top-level and in chat_template_kwargs, preserve_thinking true; tools when offered; stream true when a text callback is given. Content parts text / image_url data URLs.
Response: choices[0].message.{content, reasoning_content, tool_calls}, finish_reason; streaming only when the response is text/event-stream (data: chunks, [DONE], tool calls reassembled by index; reasoning kept apart, never returned as the answer); EOF without [DONE] keeps what arrived; stopFlag → errStopped.
Tools: up to 8 rounds; a round with calls appends the assistant turn and one tool message per call; logs ">>> <step>: round i of 8 — the model asked for name(args…)" (or "…asks for the same thing again"); exhausted → "!!! <step>: still calling tools after 8 rounds — the step gets no answer from this call"; an error on round 0 is read as "the server refused the request with tools" and the call is retried without them. Tools: web_search(broad, medium, narrow) and web_read(url) driven through headless Firefox (per-call timeout 45 s, boot 15 s, render 15 s, 8 hits, 6000 chars per page, bot-wall detection); tool errors are returned as text to the model.
Retries: transport death (EOF, reset, refused, pipe, unreachable, or gone-words) → 5 s, 20 s, 1, 2, 4 min, each cancellable; anything else → one 2 s retry; status codes are answers. Helpers: noAnswer, cutOff, thinkAgain (empty answer → thinking off), retryTurn ("Your answer failed validation: <problem>. Return corrected strict JSON only."), jsonReply (peel preamble/fence).
Stall watch: streamed calls given up after 5 min of silence; unstreamed calls capped at 10 min; heartbeat every minute (">>> <step>: nothing yet, T in" / "X thinking, Y reply, T in — “…tail”"); blame rewrites cancellations into "nothing arrived in 5m0s" / "stopped answering after T".
Gate: one request on the wire per app, taken before the watch, released after the call; ">>> <step>: waited for the LLM -- it was busy with <other>; one request at a time".
Cache: <project>/cache/llm/<step>/<sha256 of the JSON parts>; used by describe, transcript, retake, textedit, translate; only usable replies stored.
Exchange log: see shell inventory F.

## G. Invariants

1. The join is a stream copy: every clip must come out at the same frame size and audio layout (box on every clip, format on every branch, limiter on every clip); aresample before loudnorm; -t on the output for inserts/sounds/freezes/rated clips; adelay in whole ms; input indices append-only; -ss before -i; 0.5 s is the single clip floor; a dropped clip is always logged; mp4 faststart flags; outSize names the short side.
2. Cues are built for every subtitle mode; sidecars always written, stale ones deleted by exact name first; never an empty cue; translations read back by number; gaps re-asked once then left in the original; cached only when complete; own language never translated; webm cannot carry srt; placement tags written into the srt.
3. ▶ leaves an up-to-date video alone; the stamp excludes the upload text; the text is written once per project (publish.json gates it; delete publish/ to start over); publish.json is laid down before drawing; the thumbnail has its own stamp; ↻ over the picture always redraws; Own survives ▶; the title is seeded onto the picture once; re-printing words never costs a GPU run; plain bytes copied through when nothing to print.
4. The two halves touch no common file; the render's error is the run's verdict; every widget read happens on the GUI thread first; landPublish blocks until applied; the gate serialises requests per app; checkpoints only between subprocesses; writeEmbed runs even when the encode was skipped.
5. Reasoning is never the reply; empty assistant turns are not echoed; a round-0 tools error retries plainly; status codes are never "went away"; every wait is cancellable; observability never breaks the work; the cache key contains everything that decides the answer.
